  PANDUAN FOTO PER VIDEO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Shorts (60-90 detik):
  - Butuh 8-12 foto/gambar
  - Mix: 5-7 foto sejarah asli + 3-5 AI-generated
  - Sumber: Wikipedia Commons, Library of Congress,
    National Archives, archive.org, Unsplash (historical)

  Long-form (8-15 menit):
  - Butuh 25-40 foto/gambar
  - Mix: 15-25 foto asli + 10-15 AI art
  - Bonus: peta, dokumen asli, sketsa, poster propaganda

  Tips foto:
  - Resolusi minimal 1080px width (crop ke 1080x1920 portrait)
  - Foto hitam putih = drama tinggi, viewer suka
  - Close-up wajah tokoh = emotional connection
  - Peta/diagram = credibility boost
  - "Before vs After" = engagement tinggi
